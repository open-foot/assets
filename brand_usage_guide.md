# OpenFoot brand usage guide

- Website and documentation: positive lockup on Clubhouse surfaces.
- Dark navigation and editorial moments: inverse lockup.
- Compact product navigation: mark beside visible `OpenFoot` text, with the
  image hidden from assistive technology.
- Browser and installed-app contexts: generated favicon or app icon.
- Social cards: the governed social cover source with equivalent metadata.

Legacy root PNGs remain reference evidence only. They may be removed after a
tracked-source scan reports zero consumers and replacement screenshots pass.
